# Privacy Policy — Unofficial enhancements for eBird

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to
the developer or to any third party.

## What the extension does

- It reads pages on `ebird.org` that your browser is already logged in to
  (your own life-list pages), in order to determine which species are on
  your life list, current-year list, and (where applicable) county
  life/year lists.
- The results are stored only in your browser's local extension storage
  (`browser.storage.local`), on your own device.
- This data never leaves your browser. It is not sent to the developer,
  to eBird, or to any other server or third party.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
- No advertising or ad networks.
- No API keys, accounts, or sign-in are used or required by the extension
  itself.
- No data is sold, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions

- `storage`: used only to cache the list data described above, locally.
- Access to `ebird.org`: used only to read your own eBird pages so the
  extension can add status badges to species links.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this
same URL and in the extension's GitHub repository.

## Contact

Questions or concerns can be raised via GitHub issues on the project
repository.
